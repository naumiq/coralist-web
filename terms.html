2. Description of the Service

Coralist is an aquarium telemetry and tracking application created to assist saltwater and reef hobbyists in tracking water chemistry parameters (including Alkalinity, Calcium, Magnesium, Nitrate, Phosphate, Temperature, and Salinity), maintaining photographic livestock records, configuring maintenance routines, and receiving analytical telemetry guidance.
