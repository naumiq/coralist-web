Designed to simplify and support your hobby

From weekly water tests to stocking decisions at your local fish store, Coralist gives you the clarity to keep your tank thriving.

📖

Digital Logbook

No more water-stained notebooks or disconnected spreadsheets

Testing saltwater with a paper notebook nearby usually ends with wet, stained pages and lost records. Spreadsheets store numbers, but they won't help you learn from them or alert you when an alkalinity drop lines up with coral stress. Coralist keeps your water tests, livestock additions, hardware setup, and equipment maintenance together—so you can easily connect the dots and understand how changes impact your corals and fish. Your tank details are fed directly into the AI engine for analysis and advice, making every recommendation specific to your system.

🎛️

Configurable Dashboard

Your tank's vital parameters and target ranges, at a glance

Every system has different requirements — a mixed tank, LPS, or nano runs differently than a high-demand SPS reef. Coralist comes with preset reference ranges tailored for SPS-dominant, LPS-dominant, and fish-dominated tanks, so you start with sensible targets right away. Customize your dashboard with the exact parameters you care about — Alkalinity, Calcium, Nitrate, and more — and see at a glance whether your water is in the sweet spot with intuitive, color-coded dials.

📈

Stability Index Chart

Because in reef tanks, stability is king

Corals rarely suffer from a single parameter reading; they suffer from sudden swings. The Stability Index tracks your long-term trends across 1-, 3-, 6-, and 12-month windows and adjusts its scoring based on your livestock type—because what counts as "stable" for an SPS tank is different from a fish-only setup. By highlighting creeping deviations early, it helps you focus on what really matters in reef keeping: flattening the curve and keeping your water chemistry steady.

🧪

AI Water Test Analysis

Practical guidance on what your test numbers mean

Instead of wondering how a set of test results interacts, Coralist evaluates your numbers together, not in isolation. The app highlights your overall water state, explains potential impacts on your corals and invertebrates, and provides practical, grounded advice on what adjustments to make—or when it's best to leave things alone.

💬

Context-Aware Assistant

An assistant that already knows your tank and livestock

Ask questions without re-explaining your setup every time. The assistant already knows your tank volume, parameters, equipment, and livestock—so you can use it to build or adjust a dosing program, plan maintenance tasks, or check at your local fish store whether a new fish or coral is compatible with what you already have.
