Platform Architecture

Engineered for Biological Equilibrium

Built from the ground up for stable SPS, LPS, and mixed reef systems. Coralist translates complex ionic balances into intuitive stability telemetry—protecting delicate colonies and preventing creeping chemistry swings.

  • Biochemical Equilibrium Lab Precision tracking for Alkalinity (dKH), Calcium, Magnesium, and nutrient corridors with custom target bands.
  • Telemetry Stability Index Longitudinal trend curves across 1M, 3M, 6M, and 1Y to detect creeping ionic drift before coral tissue recedes.
  • Specimen, Dosing & Hardware Ledger Photographic frag growth progression, additive consumption logs, and proactive maintenance cadences.
  • Contextual Biological Advisory Real-time telemetry diagnostics that interpret ionic ratios and recommend precise adjustments.
