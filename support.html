App Support

We are here to help you get the most out of Coralist.

Contact Support

Have a question, encountered a bug, or need help managing your account and reef data? Our team is always ready to assist you.

Email Support contact@coralist.app

For general queries, bug tickets, and data requests. Response within 24–48h.

WhatsApp Business Chat on WhatsApp

Quick messaging for urgent questions or immediate feedback.

How Can We Help?

  • Technical Issues & Bug Reports: If something is not functioning properly in the app, let us know your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue.
  • Feedback & Feature Suggestions: We actively develop Coralist alongside the reefing community. Let us know what parameters, test kit integrations, or charts you would like to see next.
  • Account & Data Requests: Inquiries regarding your cloud sync, data export, or account deletion can be sent directly to our support email.
  • Subscription & Purchases: For inquiries regarding billing, store receipts, or active subscriptions managed via Apple App Store.
