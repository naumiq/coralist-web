Privacy Policy

We believe your aquarium records belong to you. This policy outlines how Coralist collects, processes, and protects your information.

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version: 1.2 • Standard: Zero Commercial Data Brokerage

1. Introduction

At Coralist, we treat user privacy with the utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information across our mobile applications, web portals, and synchronized cloud infrastructure.

2. Information We Collect

Coralist is designed to collect only the data necessary to provide accurate telemetry, livestock tracking, and maintenance management:

  • Aquarium Telemetry Data: Water chemistry test records (such as Alkalinity, Calcium, Magnesium, Nitrate, Phosphate, Temperature, Salinity) and custom dosing history.
  • Livestock Records: Coral colony species names, tags, placement notes, and photographs you choose to upload.
  • Maintenance Schedules: Custom task intervals (e.g., water change frequency, filter media changes).
  • Account Information: If you register for cloud backup, we store your email address and secure authentication tokens via Supabase.
  • Diagnostic Data: Non-personally identifiable crash reports and OS versions to ensure notification reliability.

3. Offline-First Architecture & Data Export

Coralist is engineered with an offline-first foundation:

  • All log entries, photos, and tank profiles are saved locally on your mobile device first.
  • You can utilize the core application without an internet connection and without creating an account.
  • You can export a full, unencrypted backup of all your aquarium data at any time in standard JSON and CSV formats.

ZERO DATA SELLING: Coralist does not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your water chemistry records, livestock photos, or personal contact details to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

4. Cloud Synchronization & Security

For users who enable cloud backup across devices:

  • Infrastructure: Cloud storage is powered by Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) configured to ensure only your authenticated account can access your records.
  • Encryption: Data is transmitted using TLS 1.3 encryption and stored encrypted at rest.

5. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing

When utilizing AI-assisted parameter analysis:

  • Only anonymized telemetry metrics (such as dKH, Ca, Mg numerical values) are transmitted to the AI engine (powered by Google Gemini API).
  • No personal identification, email addresses, or location data are attached to AI query payloads.
  • Your telemetry logs are not used to train public foundation models.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You hold complete authority over your stored records:

  • Local Purge: Deleting an entry or aquarium within the app immediately removes it from local device storage.
  • Account Deletion: You can initiate account deletion at any time in the app settings. Accounts scheduled for deletion enter a 30-day grace period during which you can restore your account or export data before permanent, irreversible deletion.
