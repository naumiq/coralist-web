2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create, manage, and secure your account.
  • To provide core features, including charting water parameters and reminding you of maintenance tasks.
  • To generate personalized AI-driven insights (see Section 3).
  • To process in-app purchases and manage subscription entitlements.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, security alerts, and support messages.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Data Processing

Coralist uses Google Gemini (via the Google Cloud Generative AI API) to power parameter analysis, compatibility assessments, task generation, and the conversational assistant.

  • Anonymized Data Transmission: When you request an AI analysis, we transmit non-personally identifiable aquarium data (water parameters, livestock types, equipment inventory, and your optional notes) to the Google Gemini API. Your personal information (name, email, account ID) is never included in AI prompts.
  • AI Response Flagging: If you flag an AI response as inaccurate, the conversation history and flagged reply are stored in our database linked to your user ID, for the purpose of monitoring AI quality and safety.
  • No Model Training: We access Google Gemini via its paid API tier, which is governed by the Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service and the Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy. Under these terms, data submitted via the API is not used to train or improve Google's foundation models.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information only with trusted third parties essential for operating the App. See the Sub-Processors table in Section 3 for a full list. Key details:

  • Supabase: Provides managed cloud database infrastructure and backend services hosted in the European Union (Ireland). They securely store registered user account records, encrypted/hashed credentials, and synchronized aquarium telemetry. Users authenticate directly to the Coralist service; we do not expose direct database access to end users.
  • RevenueCat: Manages our in-app purchases and subscriptions. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives anonymous app user IDs to validate subscription status. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not collect or store credit card information.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini): Processes anonymized aquarium data for AI features as described in Section 3.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in Section 2, or as required by law.

  • Active account data: Retained for the duration of your account.
  • Usage and rate-limit logs: Retained for the current calendar month plus one rolling month, then automatically purged.
  • Flagged AI responses: Retained for up to 12 months for AI quality monitoring, then deleted.
  • Backup copies: Supabase automated backups are retained for up to 7 days and are automatically overwritten.

Account Deletion Process

You may delete your account at any time directly within the App:

  1. Navigate to Settings > Account Details > Delete Account.
  2. Confirm deletion when prompted.

What happens after you request deletion:

  • Immediately: Your account is deactivated. Your data becomes inaccessible and is excluded from all active queries via Row-Level Security policies.
  • 30-day grace period: You may restore your account during this window by logging back in and selecting "Restore Account."
  • After 30 days: An automated nightly process permanently and irreversibly deletes your account and all associated data (aquarium records, logs, livestock, equipment, AI interaction history) from all active databases, including the Coralist user authentication record.

Important: Deleting your Coralist account does not cancel active App Store or Google Play subscriptions. You must cancel subscriptions separately through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

For All Users

You may exercise the following rights by contacting us at contact@coralist.app. We will respond within 30 days (or within the legally required timeframe for your jurisdiction).

  • Right to Access (Art. 15 GDPR): Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): Request deletion of your personal data (also available directly in-app).
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR): Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format. (Note: You can export your aquarium data locally via the App's backup feature.)
  • Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR): Object to processing based on legitimate interest. If you object, we will cease processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18 GDPR): Request that we limit how we use your data while a complaint is resolved.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl) or with your local supervisory authority.

Additional Rights for California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the following additional disclosures apply:

  • Categories of Personal Information Collected: Identifiers (email, name); internet or electronic network activity information (app usage data); aquarium hobbyist data.
  • Sale or Sharing of Personal Information: We do not sell or share your personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA. We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding 12 months.
  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected.
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal information (available in-app or via email).
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights.

To submit a verifiable consumer request, email contact@coralist.app with the subject line "CCPA Request."

Additional Rights for CalOPPA Compliance

  • We honor Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals. The App does not track users across third-party websites.
  • We will notify users of material Privacy Policy changes via in-app notification.

7. Children's Privacy

Coralist is a specialized reef aquarium management tool intended for a general audience of adult hobbyists and individuals aged 13 and older (or 16 and older in European Union member states where required by national legislation implementing GDPR Art. 8, such as the Netherlands).

  • No Knowingly Collected Data from Children: We do not knowingly solicit, request, or collect personal data or personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union without verifiable parental consent). The App does not intentionally target children, nor does it require users to enter their date of birth upon registration.
  • COPPA & GDPR "Actual Knowledge" Standard: Because Coralist is designed for general audiences and operates on an "actual knowledge" legal standard under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and EU GDPR Art. 8, we do not perform general age-screening or biometric age verification.
  • Parental and Guardian Takedown Mechanism: If a parent or legal guardian discovers or has reason to believe that their minor child has registered an account or provided personal information to Coralist without appropriate parental consent, they may contact us immediately at contact@coralist.app with the subject line "Children's Privacy - Parental Request". Upon receiving verifiable notification, we will promptly investigate, deactivate the account, and permanently delete all associated personal information from our active databases and servers.
  • Local-Only Guest Mode: If a minor uses the App in Local-Only Guest Mode without creating an account or logging in, all aquarium telemetry and notes remain stored solely on the physical device's local memory. No personal information or telemetry is transmitted to, processed by, or stored on our servers.
